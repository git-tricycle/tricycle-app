Flow
1. Welcome Slides (Onboarding)
-Slide 0 → "Welcome to Ride It" + tagline
-Slide 1 → "Book your Tricycle with ease"
-Slide 2 → "Just a few taps to book a ride"
-Slide 3 → "Get Started" button → proceed to Role Selection
2. Role Selection
Options:
-Student
-Driver
Kapag pinili:
-Student → Student Login
-Driver → Driver Login
3.If Student
Login Screen
-Fields: Student ID Number, Password
-Button: Login
-Link: "Don’t have an account? Register" → Student Registration
Registration (Multi-Step Form)
Split per category/slide:
-Personal Information → First Name, Middle Name, Last Name, Student ID Number, Date of Birth
-Contact Information → Mobile Number, Email
-Address Information → Province/City, Municipality, Barangay, Purok/Street
-Account Security → Username (optional), Password, Confirm Password, Profile Picture Upload
-Verification & Safety → Upload Student ID, Emergency Contact Name & Number, Terms & Privacy Checkboxes
4. If Driver
Login Screen
-Fields: Username/Email/Phone, Password
-Button: Login
-Link: "Don’t have an account? Register" → Driver Registration
Registration (Multi-Step Form)
Split per category/slide:
-Personal Information → Full Name, Age, Address, Contact Number, Email (optional)
-Account Information → Username, Password, Confirm Password
-Tricycle Information → Plate Number, Body Number, Vehicle Photo Upload, OR/CR Upload
-Verification Documents → Driver’s License Upload, Valid ID Upload
-Optional → Emergency Contact Person & Number



Tricycle Booking App – Student Features
1. Student Registration & Profile
-Sign-up with Student ID / School email.
-Profile details (name, contact number, course/year).
-Upload school ID for verification.
2. Booking a Ride
-Enter Pickup Location & Drop-off Location.
-See estimated fare.
-Request tricycle instantly.
3. Live Tracking & Driver Info
-View driver details (name, tricycle plate, photo, contact).
-Real-time map tracking (driver on the way).
-Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA).
4. Fare & Payment
-See fare estimate before confirming booking.
-Pay via:
Cash
G-Cash
-Payment receipt / transaction history.
5. Ride History
-List of past trips (date, driver, fare).
6. Ratings & Feedback
-Rate driver after ride (⭐ 1–5).
-Leave feedback for service improvement.
7. Safety Features
-SOS button (alert school admin / emergency contact).
-Share live trip link with a friend or guardian.
-Emergency contact details saved in profile.
8. Announcements & Notifications
-Updates from school/transport admin.
-Alerts (system updates, fare changes, etc.).
-Ride confirmation & reminders.
9. Student Dashboard (Home Screen)
-Quick “Book a Ride” button.
-Map preview of nearby drivers.
-Shortcut to: Ride History, Profile, Payments, Settings.
-Notifications for new announcements.



Tricycle Driver App Features
1. Driver Registration & Profile
-Driver sign-up with required documents (license, valid ID, tricycle permit, etc.)
-Profile setup (name, contact number, address, tricycle plate number, picture).
-Document verification (admin approval).
2. Driver Dashboard
-Summary of daily/weekly earnings.
-Current ride status (available, booked, in transit).
-Notifications (new bookings, payments, system updates).
3. Ride Requests Management
-Accept or decline ride requests.
-View passenger details (pickup/drop-off, name, fare estimate).
-Real-time trip updates (e.g., "On the way," "Passenger picked up," "Trip completed").
4. Navigation & Tracking
-Integrated map with route suggestion.
-GPS tracking to show passenger location.
-Route optimization (shortest path).
5. Earnings & Payment
-Daily/weekly income summary.
-Payment history (cash,G-Cash)
-Fare breakdown per trip.
6. Ratings & Feedback
-View passenger ratings.
-Respond to feedback if necessary.
-Track overall performance score.

7. Availability Control
-Toggle status: Available / Not Available.
-Auto-timeout if idle (driver offline after inactivity).
8. Notifications
-Instant ride request alerts.
-Reminders for pending payments or document updates.
-Emergency alerts (accidents, road issues).
9. Security & Support
-SOS Button (quick emergency contact).
-In-app chat or call with passenger.
-Contact support/admin directly.
10. History & Reports
-Trip history with details (date, distance, fare, passenger).
-Export reports for personal tracking.
-Cancelled or missed rides log.
